From our Customers

Things are Heating Up

Everyone has a melting point – that moment when pace, heat, and effort collide in a test of will. Engineered to excel in scorching summer temperatures, our breezy and breathable Meridian Speed Shorts are cut from a four-way stretch knit and finished with venting technology. This unique knit helps move moisture away from the skin, eliminating cling while optimizing cooling. Finished with a near-weightless liner and a shirt loop for storage. Delivered without pockets for a streamlined fit and feel during fast efforts where a phone would only slow you down.

  • Handles high temperatures and hard efforts with cling-free comfort
  • Made from a breathable, soft and UPF 50+ fabric
  • Merrow stitching on the liner and bonded hems on the shell minimize chafing
  • Luxe, light-as-air liner promises a silky soft handfeel and subtle support
  • Body Fabric: 83% Nylon, 17% Spandex, 125gsm
  • Lining Fabric: 70% Nylon, 30% Elastane, 55gsm
  • Four-way Stretch
  • Moisture-Wicking
  • Venting technology optimizes breathability and manages moisture
  • Machine wash cold with like colors on delicate cycle. Do not use bleach. Do not use softeners. Lay flat to dry. Do not iron.
  • An easy-fitting short with a 3” inseam, featuring an integrated drawstring and a split hem for maximum mobility. The liner is crafted to stay put, featuring minimal stitching that sits softly against the skin.

Key Features

Made for the Heat

Made for the miles that can make you melt, Meridian incorporates venting technology to move moisture away from the skin and to the fabric’s surface, helping it evaporate faster to keep you cool and dry for longer.

Less is More

This short is deliberately stripped back. We removed the pockets, used a featherlight liner and bonded the seams for an ultra-fast, ultra-light feel. It's designed for when speed matters, and carrying capacity doesn't.
